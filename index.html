Project Experience

Nature, under revision

Quantum Neural Circuit (QNC) Modeling, State-Update Algorithms, and Rhythmic Signal Generation

  • Built a QNC modeling and simulation framework for rhythm-compatible neural interfaces and brain-inspired temporal computing hardware, abstracting QST tunneling weight updates, QSM quantum conductance discharge, and stochastic conductive-channel generation/annihilation into circuit-level state updates.
  • Realized weight modulation, frequency adaptation, quantized pulse output, and theta-gamma phase-amplitude coupling rhythm generation; quantified system metrics including node delay (~0.76 ms), gate crosstalk (<1 pA), frequency adaptation (~150/185 Hz), PSD slope, Fano factor, SVM AUC=0.514, and memory consolidation accuracy (72.8 +/- 8.9%).
  • Took responsibility for QSM device fabrication, electrical testing, data analysis, and Verilog-A stochastic compact-model implementation, using kinetic Monte Carlo methods to reproduce conductance steps and cycle-to-cycle variations for circuit simulation and model calibration.

First-author Nature Nanotechnology paper

Polarization-Dependent Conductivity Mechanism in Layered Ferroelectrics and In-Memory Computing Applications

  • Combined theoretical calculations and experiments to reveal the microscopic mechanism of asymmetric conducting paths generated by the built-in electric field, also known as the hidden Stark effect.
  • Modulated the transistor conductivity threshold and realized electrically self-switching logic-in-memory functions including AND, OR, XOR, and NAND, providing tunable conductance elements for near-/in-memory computing.
  • Designed and fabricated dual-gate layered ferroelectric channel field-effect transistors (FeCFETs) with an asymmetric equivalent oxide thickness dielectric architecture.
  • Characterized transfer and output behavior, threshold voltage shift, hysteresis memory window, and high/low resistance states, then extracted design constraints between device-state windows and logic stability for a complex 2T1R logic network.

Nature Nanotechnology, under revision

"Electronic Spinal Cord" System Based on Neuromorphic Primitive Circuits and Motor Function Reconstruction

  • Established an ultra-low-power "brain-circuit-muscle" closed loop that addresses latency and biological adaptability limits in traditional algorithmic decoding.
  • Enabled rats to restore volition-driven forward and backward voluntary motor functions without external supervision within 14 days.
  • Constructed a Neuromorphic Primitive Circuit (NPC) integrating ferroelectric floating-gate transistors and threshold switching memristors for hardware-level biomimicry from synaptic plasticity to neuronal dynamics.
  • Led joint device design and in vivo animal experiments, and used MATLAB to process bioelectrical signals and analyze Local Field Potentials (LFP) and cross-band features in the motor cortex.
  • Evaluated input signals, circuit states, muscle stimulation outputs, and behavioral feedback as one closed-loop system, building experience for low-latency, low-power, hardware-adaptive edge neuromorphic design.
